## Usage

```ts
import "@tecton/wc/date-picker" // tec-date-picker, tec-date-range-picker (and the calendars)
import "@tecton/wc/date-field" // tec-date-field, tec-time-field
```

```html
<label for="due">Due date</label>
<tec-date-picker id="due" name="due"></tec-date-picker>

<tec-date-picker appearance="button" placeholder="Pick a date"></tec-date-picker>
```

A date picker is a [calendar](/docs/components/calendar) in a popover, opened from one of two appearances:

- **`field`** (default) — a segmented date input you can type into, with a calendar button at the end.
- **`button`** — an outline button that shows the formatted date (`date-style`, default `long`) or the `placeholder`, and opens the calendar. Put icons in the `start` / `end` slots.

Choosing a day closes the popover and returns focus. The value is an ISO date (`2026-09-26`), `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm` with a time `granularity`, or `start/end` for `<tec-date-range-picker>`; listen for `change`. Both pickers are form controls with validation.

## Composition

```text
tec-date-picker
├── field (segments + calendar button) | button (formatted value)
└── popover (role="dialog")
    ├── tec-calendar / tec-range-calendar
    └── slot="footer"
```

## Forms

Both pickers are form-associated. `required`, `min`, `max` and `isDateUnavailable` are validated (with localized messages such as "Value must be 1/1/2026 or later."), a partly typed date is `badInput`, and a reversed range is invalid. A range is submitted as `name=start/end`, or as two entries with `start-name` / `end-name`. Errors display after interaction or a submit attempt (`:state(user-invalid)`). See [Forms](/docs/forms).

<ComponentPreview name="date-picker-form" />

## Date field

`<tec-date-field>` is the segmented input on its own — no popover. Each part (month, day, year, and with a time `granularity` hour, minute, AM/PM) is a spin button in the locale's order, with localized placeholders (`mm/dd/yyyy`, `tt.mm.jjjj`). It has the `outline`, `filled` and `text` variants of [Input](/docs/components/input).

<ComponentPreview name="date-picker-date-field" />

## Time field

`<tec-time-field>` edits a time of day: `HH:mm`, or `HH:mm:ss` with `granularity="second"`. The clock follows the locale; `hour-cycle="24"` or `"12"` forces one. `min` / `max` are times.

## Accessibility

- The field is a `role="group"` of `role="spinbutton"` segments. Each segment is named after its part and the field label ("month Due date") and reports `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and `aria-valuetext` ("9 – September", or "Empty"). Separators are hidden from assistive technology.
- Label the picker with `<label for>`, `<tec-label for>` / `<tec-field-label>` or `aria-label`. The calendar button is named "Calendar" (in the picker's locale; `button-label` overrides it) plus the label; it has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`.
- The popover is a `role="dialog"` named by the label. Opening with the keyboard moves focus to the selected (or today's) day; opening with the pointer focuses the dialog without a focus ring, and the first arrow key moves into the grid. Tab stays inside, and closing returns focus to the button.
- The button appearance is a button named by the label and the shown value ("Date September 26, 2026").
- Invalid values set `aria-invalid` on the segments (or the button) once invalidity is displayed; `aria-describedby` on the element describes the field.
- Segments are `contenteditable` so on-screen keyboards open with a numeric layout; their caret is hidden.

## Usage guidelines

### Use it when

- A form needs a date (or a date range, or a date and time) and space is short: the field shows the value, the calendar is one press away.
- Users may know the date already (typing is faster) or need to see the month (picking is easier): the `field` appearance offers both.

### Not for

- a calendar that stays visible, with booked days or a schedule to read: use [Calendar](/docs/components/calendar)
- a time of day only: use `<tec-time-field>`
- a month or a year alone: use [Native Select](/docs/components/native-select)

### Do

<DoList>

<Do>

Label every picker (`<tec-field-label for>`, `<label for>` or `aria-label`), and give the format hint in a description when users type dates.

</Do>

<Do>

Prefer the `field` appearance when the date is often known (birth date, a document date); the `button` appearance suits filters and dates close to today.

</Do>

<Do>

Use `caption-layout="dropdown"` for dates far from today, such as a date of birth.

</Do>

<Do>

Constrain with `min`, `max` and `isDateUnavailable`, and say why a day is unavailable.

</Do>

<Do>

Use `<tec-date-range-picker>` for a period, not two pickers — it validates that the end is on or after the start.

</Do>

</DoList>

### Don't

<Dont severity="HIGH" title="A text input with a date parser">

<Wrong>

```html
<tec-input placeholder="June 01, 2025"></tec-input>
```

</Wrong>

<Correct>

```html
<tec-date-picker value="2025-06-01"></tec-date-picker>
```

</Correct>

A free-text date is ambiguous (is 06/01 June or January?), cannot be stepped with the keyboard and is announced as plain text. The segmented field follows the locale's order, validates each part and submits an ISO date.

</Dont>

<Dont severity="MEDIUM" title="Rebuilding the picker from a popover and a calendar">

<Wrong>

```html
<tec-popover>
  <tec-button slot="trigger">Pick a date</tec-button>
  <tec-calendar></tec-calendar>
</tec-popover>
```

</Wrong>

<Correct>

```html
<tec-date-picker appearance="button" name="due"></tec-date-picker>
```

</Correct>

The hand-built version needs script to format the value, close on selection and move focus, and it takes no part in forms. `<tec-date-picker>` does all of that and validates.

</Dont>
