## Usage

```ts
import "@tecton/wc/panel"
```

```html
<tec-panel>
  <tec-panel-header>
    <tec-panel-title>Well properties</tec-panel-title>
    <tec-panel-actions>…</tec-panel-actions>
  </tec-panel-header>
  <tec-panel-content>…</tec-panel-content>
  <tec-panel-footer>…</tec-panel-footer>
</tec-panel>
```

<Callout>
  [Card](/docs/components/card) is for content; `<tec-panel>` is the application chrome around a tool: it fills its container, scrolls its body and pins its footer.
</Callout>

## Composition

```text
tec-panel
├── tec-panel-header
│   ├── tec-panel-title
│   ├── tec-panel-description
│   └── tec-panel-actions
├── tec-panel-content
└── tec-panel-footer
```

## Variants

Use the `variant` attribute: `default` with a hairline border, `elevated` with a shadow, `flat` without an edge and `outline` with a visible border on a transparent background.

## Sizes

Use the `size` attribute to scale the padding of header, content and footer (`--tec-panel-px` / `--tec-panel-py`).

<ComponentPreview name="panel-sizes" />

## Footer

Give the panel a height: `<tec-panel-content>` scrolls and `<tec-panel-footer>` stays pinned to the bottom. Every part is the layout box of its children, so layout classes go on the part itself: `class="flex flex-col gap-4"` on the content, `class="justify-end"` on the footer (or its `justify` attribute).

<ComponentPreview name="panel-footer" />

## Many actions

`<tec-panel-actions>` is a plain slot: one to three icon buttons beside a title that truncates. When a panel carries more, put an [Overflow](/docs/tecton/overflow) toolbar inside the slot. The title then keeps its natural width up to 60% of the header, the slot takes the rest, and the actions move into a More menu lowest priority first. A custom More button (`slot="menu-trigger"`) keeps the panel's small button size. Panels that do not opt in pay nothing.

## Keyboard interactions

The panel is not interactive; focus and keys belong to the controls in it. An overflow toolbar in `<tec-panel-actions>` is one tab stop with arrow-key navigation (see [Overflow](/docs/tecton/overflow#keyboard-interactions)).

## Accessibility

- `<tec-panel-title>` is a heading of level 2 (`level` changes it to fit the page outline).
- `<tec-panel-description>` is a paragraph. Icon buttons in `<tec-panel-actions>` need an `aria-label`.
- The panel renders a `section` without a name, so it is not a landmark. Put it in a named region (for example an `aside` with an `aria-label`) when it is one of the page's main areas.
- Borders stay visible in forced-colours mode.

## Usage guidelines

### Use it when

- A tool surface inside the application: a properties panel, an inspector, a dashboard section.
- The panel is given a height by its container and its body has to scroll inside it.
- Actions belong to the panel itself — in the header row, or pinned at the bottom.

### Not for

- a self-contained content block that grows with its text: use [Card](/docs/components/card)
- the title block at the top of a page: use [Page Header](/docs/tecton/page-header)
- a panel that floats over the page and is dismissed: use [Sheet](/docs/components/sheet)
- the application frame around the work area: use [App Shell](/docs/tecton/app-shell)

### Do

<DoList>

<Do>

Compose it: `<tec-panel-header>` with `<tec-panel-title>`, `<tec-panel-description>` and `<tec-panel-actions>`, then `<tec-panel-content>`, then `<tec-panel-footer>`.

</Do>

<Do>

Choose the surface with `variant="default" | "elevated" | "flat" | "outline"` and the padding with `size="sm" | "md" | "lg"`.

</Do>

<Do>

Put the body in `<tec-panel-content>`: it is the part that takes the remaining height and scrolls.

</Do>

<Do>

Give the panel its height from the outside (`class="h-72"`, or an app shell aside); the parts handle the rest.

</Do>

<Do>

Keep one to three icon buttons in `<tec-panel-actions>`; beyond that put a `<tec-toolbar>` inside the slot and the title yields width to it.

</Do>

</DoList>

### Don't

<Dont severity="HIGH" title="A body that is not in tec-panel-content">

<Wrong>

```html
<tec-panel class="h-72">
  <tec-panel-header><tec-panel-title>Well properties</tec-panel-title></tec-panel-header>
  <div class="p-4">…</div>
  <tec-panel-footer><tec-button size="sm">Open</tec-button></tec-panel-footer>
</tec-panel>
```

</Wrong>

<Correct>

```html
<tec-panel class="h-72">
  <tec-panel-header><tec-panel-title>Well properties</tec-panel-title></tec-panel-header>
  <tec-panel-content>…</tec-panel-content>
  <tec-panel-footer><tec-button size="sm">Open</tec-button></tec-panel-footer>
</tec-panel>
```

</Correct>

Only `<tec-panel-content>` takes the remaining height and scrolls, so a plain `div` in its place is clipped instead of scrolling and pushes the footer out of view.

</Dont>

<Dont severity="MEDIUM" title="Painting the variant by hand">

<Wrong>

```html
<tec-panel class="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 bg-white shadow-md">…</tec-panel>
```

</Wrong>

<Correct>

```html
<tec-panel variant="elevated" size="lg">…</tec-panel>
```

</Correct>

The shadow is the `elevated` variant and the padding is the `size` scale, which the parts read through `--tec-panel-px` / `--tec-panel-py`. `border-gray-200` and `bg-white` are stock Tailwind colours and emit no CSS with the Tecton palette, and a border on the element draws a second edge outside the panel's own.

</Dont>
