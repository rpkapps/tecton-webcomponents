---
title: Tecton components
description: Components that exist only in the Tecton design system and have no shadcn/ui counterpart.
---

The Tecton theme and the Tecton variants of the standard components cover most of the design system. What remains here are the patterns shadcn/ui does not have at all — a selectable chip group, a segmented meter, a tree view, KPI stats, panels and the application shell. They follow the same conventions as every other element: attributes and slots, `::part()` hooks, `--tec-*` variables and the WAI-ARIA pattern that fits.
